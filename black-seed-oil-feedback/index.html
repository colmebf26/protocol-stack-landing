Creative Feedback

Consolidated design and copy feedback for the current Black Seed Oil launch assets, with the exact changes that should be made before finalizing.

Creative 01

“How To Take” Graphic

The black-on-black direction is premium, masculine, and on-brand. The dosage is instantly understandable, but the product needs to be identified faster and the bottom claims need cleaner wording.

Keep

What is working

  • Black-on-black premium aesthetic.
  • Large “2” makes dosage immediately clear.
  • Strong hierarchy and breathing room.
  • Amber softgels add useful warmth and contrast.
  • “2 SOFTGELS / ONCE A DAY” is very clear.
Change

Design fixes

  • Make the Black Seed Oil product identity more obvious: add the bottle, logo, or “BLACK FOREST BLACK SEED OIL.”
  • The glass is slightly too dominant; shift more attention to the product.
  • Make the softgels roughly 30–50% larger for mobile.
  • Reduce the visual dominance of “HOW TO TAKE” slightly.
Copy

Instruction copy

2 SOFTGELS
ONCE DAILY

with your largest meal and a full glass of water.

Copy

Benefit wording

Replace the existing three bottom claims with:

  • GUT HEALTH SUPPORT*
  • IMMUNE HEALTH SUPPORT*
  • ANTIOXIDANT DEFENSE*
Creative 02

Product / Bottle Visual

The bottle design already feels premium, botanical, dark, and very Black Forest. The biggest opportunity is to show the actual delivery format more clearly.

Keep

What is working

  • Strong black label and premium visual language.
  • “ETHIOPIAN BLACK SEED OIL” has strong shelf impact.
  • Cold-pressed and thymoquinone cues give the product specificity.
  • The visual feels masculine and high-end.
Add

Actual softgels

  • Add 2–3 real-looking amber/golden softgels.
  • Make them glossy and reflective, not flat or opaque.
  • Place them naturally near the bottle base / foreground.
  • Make them large enough to be unmistakable on mobile.
  • Do not scatter too many or let them compete with the bottle.
Expected Impact

What the softgels do for the design

Instant comprehension

People immediately understand that this is a softgel supplement rather than a loose oil, seed, or extract.

Better contrast

The amber/golden softgels break up the black palette and make the visual more dimensional.

More commercial

Showing the actual delivery form makes the image feel like a finished consumer product rather than only a conceptual ingredient shot.

Higher score

Current design: 8.8/10.
With well-executed softgels: 9.3–9.5/10.

Final direction

Make the bottle the hero. Use the softgels as proof of format, not decoration.
  • 2–3 glossy amber softgels.
  • Natural placement near the bottle.
  • Large enough for mobile.
  • Keep the black premium aesthetic.
  • Avoid extra clutter.
