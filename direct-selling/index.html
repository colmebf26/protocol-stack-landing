Black Forest • Direct Selling Launch

Launch Plan & Critical Path

Discussion framework for turning the existing Black Forest Supplements + Black Forest MD ecosystem into a direct-selling channel — without treating this like a company being built from zero.

The critical path: close the legal model → finalize the Brand Partner offer and compensation → select/configure software → build the go-to-market system → recruit the founding group → soft launch → scale.

Timeline

Target soft launch: October 1

Aug 17–28
Phase 1

Close the Legal + Business Architecture

This is the gating item. Everything downstream should be built around the final answer.

Decisions to close

  • What can Brand Partners promote?
  • Supplements: commissionable?
  • Membership/platform/coaching: commissionable?
  • Prescription products: commissionable or noncommissionable?
  • Can Brand Partners refer customers into Black Forest MD?
  • Can compensation arise when a referred customer later becomes an Rx patient?

Money + compliance

  • What counts toward Commissionable Value?
  • What does not count toward Commissionable Value?
  • Which entity receives each part of customer payment?
  • What may Brand Partners say about peptides/Rx?
  • What information may Brand Partners access?
  • 1099/W-2 and state-specific implications.
Deliverable: one approved diagram showing customer attribution, Black Forest / MD / physician / pharmacy flow, and how every dollar is treated for commissions.
Aug 24–Sep 4
Phase 2

Finalize the Brand Partner Offer + Starter Kit

Turn the existing starter-kit concept into the actual offer someone can buy and understand in 30 seconds.

Enrollment

  • Enrollment / Starter Kit price.
  • Whether the kit is mandatory.
  • Whether product purchase is mandatory.
  • What a Brand Partner receives immediately.
  • Required compliance/training before promotion.

Commercial offer

  • Customer, Preferred Customer and Brand Partner pricing.
  • Autoship/subscription structure.
  • Optional product packs.
  • Membership structure if used.
  • Commissionable Value for every commissionable item.
  • Founder Program concept.
Deliverable: final Brand Partner offer + complete Starter Kit contents + initial product/price/Commissionable Value matrix.
Aug 17–Sep 8
Phase 3

Select the Direct-Selling Software

Vendor demos can begin immediately; final configuration depends on the approved legal/compensation model.

Must support

  • Brand Partner + customer enrollment.
  • Sponsor attribution and genealogy/downline.
  • Commissionable Value and noncommissionable products.
  • Ranks and compensation engine.
  • Replicated sites/referral links.
  • Autoship/subscriptions.

Company controls

  • Commission statements.
  • Refund/clawback handling.
  • KYC/tax reporting.
  • Training/content library.
  • Reporting and compliance.
  • Shopify/API + Black Forest MD funnel attribution where needed.
Dates: demos/shortlist by Aug 28 → negotiate by Sep 4 → sign vendor by Sep 8.
Aug 28–Sep 12
Phase 4

Finalize the Compensation Plan

Once legal defines what can generate commissions, design the actual economics.

Plan design

  • Retail and Preferred Customer commissions.
  • Sponsor / Fast Start bonuses.
  • Team commissions and ranks.
  • Founder incentives.
  • Personal/team volume requirements.
  • Commissionable Value per SKU.
  • Refund and cancellation clawbacks.

Unit economics

  • COGS.
  • Fulfillment.
  • Payment processing.
  • Commission payout.
  • Support costs.
  • Acquisition costs.
  • Black Forest contribution margin.
Deliverable: Compensation Plan V1 by Sep 5 → final legal/economic version by Sep 12.
Aug 28–Sep 15
Phase 5

Build the Go-To-Market + Founder Program

The question is not “how do we launch an MLM?” It is: why would a strong seller or creator choose Black Forest to build with?

Recruiting pools

  • Existing Black Forest affiliates.
  • Existing creators/ambassadors.
  • High-value customers/referrers.
  • Supplement, fitness, wellness, biohacking and longevity creators.
  • Experienced direct sellers/team builders.

Build the pitch

  • Why Black Forest?
  • Why now?
  • Why the product ecosystem is differentiated.
  • Why Black Forest MD adds strategic value.
  • Founder Program positioning and incentives.
  • Initial recruiting funnel and outreach plan.
Deliverables: GTM strategy by Sep 8 → Founder Program by Sep 12 → recruiting list + funnel ready by Sep 15.
Sep 5–22
Phase 6

Produce the Field Materials + Training

Build only what is required to recruit, activate and keep the first Brand Partners compliant.

Recruiting assets

  • Opportunity landing page.
  • 10–15 minute opportunity presentation/video.
  • Founder Program page.
  • Recruiting scripts + FAQ.
  • Compensation explainer.
  • Objection handling.
  • Join page.

Training

  • Getting Started / first customer / first recruit.
  • Product education.
  • Compensation plan.
  • Social media.
  • Product-claims compliance.
  • Income-claims compliance.
  • Peptide/Rx boundaries.
  • Duplication + leadership.
Deliverable: V1 Starter Kit + recruiting + training ecosystem live by Sep 22. Sophisticated AI/onboarding features can be phased in after V1.
Sep 8–25
Phase 7

Configure + Test the Software

Configure products, pricing, Commissionable Value, compensation, ranks, autoship, referral attribution, payments, training and reporting.

Required test scenario

  • A Brand Partner joins and recruits another Brand Partner.
  • Customers buy supplements and subscriptions.
  • A referred customer enters the Black Forest MD flow.
  • A transaction is refunded.
  • A partner ranks up.
  • Verify every resulting dollar, commission, statement and clawback.
Deliverable: end-to-end operational platform by Sep 25.
Sep 15–30
Phase 8

Recruit the Founding 25–50

Build a closed founding cohort before opening the program broadly.

Founding cohort

  • Select/recruit 25–50 high-quality Brand Partners.
  • Enroll and train them.
  • Complete required compliance certification.
  • Teach product, scripts and compensation.
  • Have them use the back office and build their first prospect lists.
  • Collect objections and friction before launch.
Deliverable: trained founding group ready for live activity on Oct 1.
Oct 1–15
Phase 9

Soft Launch + Prove the Model

Do not scale until the founding group has actually sold, recruited, subscribed customers, earned commissions and used support.

Run live

  • Recruiting and customer acquisition.
  • Supplement sales.
  • Subscriptions/autoship.
  • MD referral/handoff where appropriate.
  • Commissions and rank movement.
  • Payouts, refunds and customer support.

Measure

  • Partner activation.
  • Customers / sales per partner.
  • Recruiting rate.
  • Subscription conversion + retention.
  • Refund rate.
  • Commission payout % and gross margin.
  • Compliance/software/support issues.
Decision: Oct 15 GO / FIX / NO-GO review before scaling.
Oct 15+
Phase 10

Scale What Works

Expand only after the economics, compliance, software and field behavior work in the real world.

Scale sequence

  • 25–50 founders → 100 → 250 → 500 → broader launch.
  • Expand creator/affiliate recruitment.
  • Recruit experienced field leaders.
  • Add webinars, events and paid recruiting only after conversion is proven.
  • Continue tightening onboarding, software, support and compliance.
